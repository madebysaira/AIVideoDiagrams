# Loop

**Best for:** reinforcing cycles — the generate → review → refine flywheel, a content calendar that feeds itself, a training loop that improves with every delivery.

## Layout conventions
- Stations arranged around a central hub in a circle. Hub is `focal` (shared memory: style guide, character bible, brand tokens).
- Stations are `primary` rects at compass points; the cycle reads clockwise.
- Outer ring: the forward arrows (`edge-accent`). Inner ring: the write-backs to the hub (`edge-link` dashed) with labels ("log what worked", "append to bible").
- Each arrow gets a one-word `arrow-label`: generate, review, refine, deliver.
- Keep to 4–6 stations. A loop with 8 stations is a wheel, not a diagram.

## Anti-patterns
- Arrows that don't close the loop. If the last step doesn't feed the first, it's not a loop, it's a pipeline.
- The hub drawn as just another station. The hub is different: it accumulates state.
- More than two rings (forward + write-back). A third ring means the diagram is trying to be a sequence too.
- Stations at random angles. Compass points (N, E, S, W + diagonals) only.
