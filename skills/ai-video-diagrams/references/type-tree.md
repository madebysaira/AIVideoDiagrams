# Tree

**Best for:** parent → children structure — project folder layout, shot breakdown, asset hierarchy, a channel's content pillars.

## Layout conventions
- Root at top, children descending. Connectors are orthogonal: vertical drop from the parent, then horizontal to each child.
- Each level uses one treatment: root `focal`, first level `primary`, deeper levels `input` or `store` by role.
- `sublabel`s carry counts or types ("12 shots", "mp4", "r3d").
- Keep depth to 3 levels and width to ~9 children at the widest level. Deeper means the hierarchy needs its own document.

## Anti-patterns
- Children hanging at different depths on the same level. Align each level to one baseline.
- More than one root. Two roots means two trees.
- Connectors drawn through the middle of child boxes. Drop to the top edge, then horizontal.
- Emoji folder icons. Mono sublabels say "mp4" better than a picture of a film.
