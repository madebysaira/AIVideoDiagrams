# Funnel

**Best for:** ranked hierarchy or drop-off — concept funnel (30 ideas → 3 pitches → 1 delivered), client tiers, review passes that eliminate candidates.

## Layout conventions
- Widest at top, narrowing down. Each tier is a centered bar with width proportional to the quantity; `sublabel` carries the number and the reason it survives ("score > 7", "client picked").
- Tier labels on the left, `eyebrow` style. The narrowing should read as a story, not a graph.
- The surviving tier (the delivered one) is `focal`. Eliminated material is `store` or `danger` only if the funnel is about failure (review passes).
- Keep to 4–6 tiers. A funnel with 9 tiers is a bar chart pretending.

## Anti-patterns
- Equal-width tiers. The shape carries the drop-off; equal widths erase it.
- Percentages that don't compound (each tier's number must follow from the one above).
- A tier wider than the one above it. Funnels narrow; if the audience grows, it's a pyramid or a pipeline.
- Decorative 3D cone edges. Flat bars, centered.
